Intracellular promoter screening

16 Apr 2026

Experiment ID
WL-EX-001
Researcher
PREVAIL Wet Lab Team
Project module
Intracellular expression control
Status
Completed

Objective

To compare candidate promoters and identify promoters with intracellular-specific responsiveness after bacterial invasion of tumor cells.

Materials

  • Engineered bacterial strains carrying promoter-reporter constructs
  • Tumor cell culture model
  • Cell culture medium, antibiotics, and PBS
  • Fluorescence imaging and signal-quantification workflow

Controls

  • Non-invaded bacterial culture
  • Reporter-only baseline control
  • Constitutive promoter reference

Procedure summary

  1. Prepare bacterial strains with the candidate promoter-reporter constructs.
  2. Co-incubate bacteria with tumor cells under the planned infection conditions.
  3. Remove extracellular bacteria according to the experimental workflow.
  4. Measure reporter signals from intracellular and extracellular conditions.
  5. Compare signal strength and leakage to select candidates for follow-up validation.
