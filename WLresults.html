03 Interpretation & Limitations

  • SupportedState only what the reported data directly support.
  • UnresolvedList confounders, missing controls, model limitations, and unanswered mechanism questions.
  • Negative resultsDocument failed constructs and inconclusive assays when they informed engineering decisions.
  • Next testName the experiment that would most reduce the remaining uncertainty.
