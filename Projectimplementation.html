01 Implementation

This page is ready for implementation planning and deployment context.

Implementation should connect the engineered system to a realistic use context without presenting an early research prototype as a clinical product.

Example structure — validate each assumption with clinicians, biosafety specialists, manufacturing experts, patients, and regulators before replacing these prompts.

02 Proposed Users & Use Context

Clinical

Care teams

Describe who would administer or monitor the intervention, the intended treatment setting, and the evidence they would need.

Patient

Eligible populations

Define inclusion assumptions carefully and discuss how pre-existing immunity, tumor type, and patient variability affect suitability.

System

Manufacturing & oversight

Map production, quality control, containment, distribution, monitoring, and responsible disposal requirements.

03 Translation Roadmap

Preclinical validation

Define efficacy, biodistribution, dose, persistence, shedding, and toxicity evidence required before translation.

Containment and manufacturing

Specify genetic stability, kill-switch validation, batch consistency, storage, transport, and waste handling.

Ethics and regulation

Document consent, risk communication, equitable access, regulatory pathway, and long-term surveillance questions.
