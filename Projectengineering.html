02 Design–Build–Test–Learn

Design

Define the engineering question

State the required function, design constraints, expected behavior, and the measurement that will determine success.

Build

Document the construct

Record the chassis, genetic parts, assembly method, sequence references, and links to Registry documentation.

Test

Measure against controls

Show the protocol, biological and technical replicates, controls, raw-data access, and analysis method.

Learn

Turn evidence into a decision

Explain what worked, what failed, the limitation revealed, and exactly what changed in the next iteration.
