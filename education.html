01 Education

This page is ready for education and outreach records.

Education documentation should identify learners, define a learning objective, show what was delivered, evaluate learning or engagement, and explain how the activity improved.

Example structure — use real participant counts, consent and safeguarding procedures, accessible materials, evaluation instruments, and honest outcomes.

02 Learning Design

Audience

Understand the learners

Define age, prior knowledge, language, accessibility needs, cultural context, recruitment, and safeguarding requirements.

Objective

Specify the outcome

Write an observable learning objective rather than a broad intention to raise awareness.

Activity

Align the experience

Show how the format, materials, facilitation, and assessment support the objective and invite two-way engagement.
