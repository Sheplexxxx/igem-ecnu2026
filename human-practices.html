01 Integrated Human Practices

This page is ready for human practices documentation.

Integrated Human Practices should show how values and stakeholder knowledge changed the project's design, experiments, implementation, communication, or risk management.

Example structure — obtain consent, describe stakeholder expertise and limitations, preserve accurate records, and connect every consultation to a documented project decision.

02 Stakeholder Map
