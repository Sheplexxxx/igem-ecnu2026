The Wiki safety page should explain project-specific hazards, design choices, laboratory controls, and responsible-use considerations without copying confidential safety forms.

Example structure — replace prompts with the team's approved risk assessment, institutional procedures, training records, containment evidence, and current iGEM safety-form references.

02 Safe Project Design

Biological

Chassis and genetic parts

Document organism risk group, strain properties, virulence-related features, antimicrobial resistance, payload risks, and safer alternatives considered.

Containment

Prevent unintended survival

Describe physical and genetic containment, kill-switch logic, escape assumptions, validation conditions, and known failure modes.

Application

Responsible deployment

Assess shedding, persistence, horizontal transfer, vulnerable populations, dual-use concerns, monitoring, and disposal.
